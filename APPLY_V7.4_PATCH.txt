1. Stop Streamlit with Ctrl+C.
2. Copy app.py and the medclaim folder from this patch into your medclaim-sentinel project.
3. Choose Replace the files in the destination.
4. Do not delete your storage folder.
5. Start with:
   powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_windows.ps1 -Port 8502
6. Open Dashboard and expand "Delete or clean up claim entries".
